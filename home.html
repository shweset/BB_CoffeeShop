A Place for Coffee Lovers

Bean Boutique brings coffee lovers together through unique blends and single-origin coffee beans. We help you explore different flavours and find coffee for your favourite brewing method.

We also sell brewing equipment, from French presses to espresso machines, to help you prepare coffee at home. Our tasting sessions and workshops offer a chance to learn new skills and meet other coffee lovers.
